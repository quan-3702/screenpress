ScreenPress privacy policy

ScreenPress works entirely on your own computer. Your screen, camera and microphone are used only while you record, and only after you allow it. Recordings are kept only on your PC (in the app's own storage) and in files you choose to save. Nothing is uploaded, collected or shared.

The app has no account, no advertising and no tracking.

Purchases of ScreenPress Pro are handled by the Microsoft Store.
