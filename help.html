How to use ScreenPress

1. Choose what to record

Screen + camera puts your face in a bubble in a corner of the video – great for lessons, tutorials and presentations. Screen only records just the screen, Camera only records just you. Choose the camera size and corner; you can move the camera to another corner while you record.

2. Sound

Choose your microphone – the green bar moves when it hears you. Tick Include computer sound to record the sound of videos, music or a call too. When Windows asks what to share, choose a screen and tick Also share system audio (or “Share tab audio” for a browser tab).

3. Record

Click Start recording, choose the screen, window or browser tab, then Share. After the 3‑2‑1 countdown, go to the window you want to show – ScreenPress keeps recording in the background. Use ⧉ Pop out controls for a small timer and stop button that stays on top of other windows. Pause and resume as often as you like. Click ■ Stop (or “Stop sharing” in the Windows bar) when you are done.

4. Trim, cut and save

After recording the video opens in the editor. Click the timeline to jump, drag on it to select a part, then Remove selection. Trim start here and Trim end here remove the beginning or end. Space plays and pauses, Delete removes the selection, Ctrl+Z undoes. Then click Save video… and choose where to save the file. Saving an edited video takes about as long as the video plays.

Your recordings stay in My recordings on this PC until you delete them. You can also open any MP4 or WebM video with Open a video to trim it.

5. Meeting notes – what was said and who said it

Record an online meeting (tick Include computer sound so the other people are recorded too), or open a recording. Then click 📝 Notes on the recording (or 📝 Meeting notes… in the editor).

  1. Choose the Spoken language and the Quality. For Vietnamese and most other languages choose Very accurate or Most accurate (Pro).
  2. Click ✍ Write down what was said. The words appear with the time. Click a time to hear that moment, click a line to fix a word.
  3. Click 👥 Find who is speaking (Pro). If you know how many people talk, choose the number first. Type the names – ▶ plays each person. One person split in two? Use Same as….
  4. Click 🧠 Remember voices: in the next meeting the names are filled in by themselves (people named in CaptionPress are recognised too).
  5. Save 📄 Meeting minutes – Word (Pro: date, participants, how long each spoke, space for decisions, the full transcript with names) or a Transcript (.txt).

The AI runs on this PC: the first time it downloads once (80–750 MB depending on the quality), then it works offline. The meeting is never uploaded. Please tell people when you record them.

Free and Pro

Free: recordings up to 5 minutes in HD 720p, WebM videos, trimming the start and end, a small “Recorded with ScreenPress” mark. Pro (one-time purchase, no subscription): record as long as you like, no mark, Full HD / 2K / 4K and 60 frames per second, MP4 videos, cutting out parts in the middle, a title at the start, rounded or square camera with a coloured ring, and meeting notes: the whole meeting written down in every quality, who said what, remembered voices and Word minutes (free: Fast quality, the first 5 minutes, transcript as a text file).

Problems?

Camera or microphone not working: open Windows Settings → Privacy & security → Camera (or Microphone) and allow apps to use it. Close Teams, Zoom or the Camera app if they are using the camera.

No computer sound: tick “Also share system audio” when Windows asks what to share.
